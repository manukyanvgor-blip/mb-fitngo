About Melkon

At MB Fit&Go, we believe fitness shouldn’t feel like a chore, it should feel like freedom. Founded by expert personal trainer Melkon Boudakian, our mission is to bridge the gap between high-performance strength and fluid mobility.

We don’t just train for aesthetics; we train for life, ensuring your body moves as well as it looks. Whether you're an athlete looking to improve performance or someone who wants to move pain-free in everyday life, our customized approach will help you reach your goals.

Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need a gym membership?

Most of our TrainHeroic programs require access to standard gym equipment (dumbbells, barbells, bands). However, we also offer bodyweight and minimal-equipment options.

How does TrainHeroic work?

TrainHeroic is an app where you will access your daily workouts, log your weights, view exercise demo videos, and track your progress over time.

Is this for beginners?

Absolutely. We have programs designed specifically for beginners focusing on form, mobility, and building a strong foundation.
