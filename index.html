About Melkon

At MB Fit&Go, we believe fitness shouldn’t feel like a chore, it should feel like freedom. Founded by expert personal trainer Melkon Boudakian, our mission is to bridge the gap between high-performance strength and fluid mobility.

We don’t just train for aesthetics; we train for life, ensuring your body moves as well as it looks. Whether you're an athlete looking to improve performance or someone who wants to move pain-free in everyday life, our customized approach will help you reach your goals.
